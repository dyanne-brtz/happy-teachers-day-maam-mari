Happy Teachers' Day, Ma'am Mari!

A simple tribute from your students

Dear Ma'am Mari,

Thank you, Ma'am, for teaching us the world of programming and web development. Before your class, we didn't even know how to write a single line of HTML. Now, we can create our own websites using VS Code — and that's because of you. You made coding feel less scary and more exciting.

Thank you for your patience every time our code doesn't work and we get errors we don't understand. You always guide us step by step — from fixing broken tags, to debugging CSS, to finally seeing our website come to life in the browser. You never gave up on us, even when our code refused to cooperate.

You didn't just teach us how to code — you taught us how to think, how to solve problems, and how to keep trying even when things get hard. Those lessons go beyond the four walls of the computer lab. Because of you, we now believe we can build anything we set our minds to.

On this special day, we just want you to know that every line of code we write, every website we build, and every bug we fix — is a reflection of what you taught us. Thank you for being our teacher, our guide, and our biggest inspiration in ITE.

Happy Teachers' Day, Ma'am! We love you! 💗
